title: "Give a Classroom Question <strong>Some Time</strong>"
items:
  - number: "01"
    title: "Start With What the Class Is Asking"
    content: "Let the teacher connect a learner's question to the lesson. The circuit demo gives an example: how does resistance relate to current?"
  - number: "02"
    title: "Pause Before the First Change"
    content: "Ask learners what they expect to happen. Let them write it down or say it aloud, with time to explain their reason."
  - number: "03"
    title: "Notice What Changes"
    content: "Choose one condition to investigate and look carefully at the observation. Compare it with the prediction and discuss how the model relates to the scientific idea."
  - number: "04"
    title: "Hear More Than One Explanation"
    content: "Invite a learner to describe what they noticed, then ask classmates what they would add. The teacher guides discussion and clarifies the limits of the simulation."
  - number: "05"
    title: "Leave With a Question to Revisit"
    content: "Give the part that still puzzles the class another look. Let the discussion help you choose the focus of the next attempt."
