items:
  - question: "What can I see in the VLABS demo?"
    answer: "The student demo shows Home, Assignments, Virtual Lab, Practice and Progress. Featured Physics 10 labs include projectile motion, an Ohm's law circuit and a conical pendulum/centripetal-force experiment. Screenshots show a demo workspace; displayed figures are sample data."
  - question: "Is VLABS only for physics?"
    answer: "VLABS is a platform for science practice across physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics and other science subjects. The demo shown here uses mechanics and electricity examples, with guided 2D and 3D experiments. Confirm the experiments, publishing and assessment workflow available for your intended use. Other subject coverage, AI assistance, adaptive assessment, advanced analytics and integrations need separate confirmation."
  - question: "Why are some demo learning areas empty?"
    answer: "The student demo has no teacher-published Practice work, and its competence and graded-work areas are empty. The teacher demo also has empty lesson, own-lab and marking areas. We kept those states in the screenshots rather than staging lessons or grades. Displayed activity figures are sample data, not evidence of learning gains."
  - question: "How do virtual labs fit with physical practice?"
    answer: "Use virtual exploration for preparation, repetition and discussion alongside supervised physical practical work. Teachers decide the right combination for the topic and the class. The simulation's accuracy, limits and the safety requirements of the real experiment need to be made clear."
  - question: "Where does the teacher come in?"
    answer: "Teachers know the class and set the learning goal. They help learners talk through a prediction, make sense of an observation and decide what needs another look. Any AI-assisted material or automated recommendation, if provided, needs professional review before use."
  - question: "How do different classroom needs shape VLABS?"
    answer: "We want science practice to have a place in classrooms with different circumstances. Talk with us about shared devices and the guidance learners need to take part. Review connectivity, device compatibility and accessibility needs for each setting. This is our educational ambition, not a measured social-impact claim."
  - question: "How can our school begin?"
    answer: "Request a Demo and bring a question from your science lessons. Tell us who is learning and what you would like to explore. Your enquiry starts a conversation; the team confirms the demonstration and any next steps separately. Sending the form does not create a learning account."
  - question: "What should we confirm before classroom use?"
    answer: "Agree content, available functions, devices, teacher support and evaluation criteria. Confirm data purposes, access, retention and each party's responsibilities. Reference prices are not a confirmed offer; timing, service terms and payment availability also need agreement with VLABS."
