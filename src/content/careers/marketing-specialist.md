## Illustrative Role: Explain the Work

Educators need copy that shows what happens in a lesson, not a list of grand claims. This illustrative profile describes product communication for the VLABS development direction, not a confirmed vacancy or employment offer. It is not an open position.

## Editorial Work This Role Could Support

- Write from the teaching moment: a prediction, a practical choice and the evidence a teacher reviews.
- Build clear briefs for teachers, school leaders and training teams, reflecting their different decisions.
- Separate development intent, agreed pilot scope and verified availability in every product claim.
- Use enquiry themes and content feedback to find missing explanations, not to imply adoption or benefits.
- Publish customer statements or quantitative claims only with evidence, approval and permission.

## Professional Experience to Discuss

Relevant experience would include education writing, audience research and content coordination, with examples of how analytics informed an editorial decision. The tools, channels and qualifications for any actual role require a confirmed brief.
