## Illustrative Role: Make Information Findable

Finding a page is only useful if its answer helps an educator make a decision. This illustrative profile describes possible content and product work for VLABS, not a confirmed vacancy or current staffing arrangement. It is not an open position.

## Content Work This Role Could Support

- Organise information around teaching needs, institutional decisions and the steps of a practical learning workflow.
- Review navigation, metadata and search paths so readers reach a useful answer, not another vague product page.
- Keep the development roadmap distinct from verified availability through coordinated product and engineering review.
- Align bilingual information while keeping illustrative scenarios clearly separate from customer evidence.
- Read search and content feedback critically; attention alone is not evidence of educational impact.

## Professional Experience to Discuss

Relevant experience includes content strategy, search fundamentals, product coordination and information architecture, with a clear rationale for each editorial choice. The scope, tools and qualifications for an actual role require confirmation with the team.
