## Illustrative Role: Design for Real Conditions

A learner should not lose a practical observation when a connection falters. This illustrative profile explores frontend work that could support the VLABS development direction, not a confirmed vacancy or final technology stack. It is not an open position.

## Engineering Work This Role Could Support

- Translate lessons, practical tasks and progress review into responsive interfaces that preserve context across devices.
- Build reusable components with explicit loading, empty, error and recovery states, including interrupted submissions.
- Test keyboard paths, assistive-technology behaviour and performance on representative learning devices, not only a development machine.
- Show the reasons and limits of planned AI recommendations, with teacher controls to review, adjust or reject them.
- Agree reliable data interactions with design, education and backend specialists, then review pilot feedback.

## Professional Experience to Discuss

Relevant experience includes JavaScript or TypeScript, semantic HTML, CSS, component architecture, browser testing and accessibility, with examples of designing recoverable tasks. Any framework choices, seniority expectations and technical requirements must be confirmed in an actual role description.
