## Illustrative Role: Make Decisions Visible

Learners must know what to change, what to record and how to recover from a mistake. This illustrative profile explores research and interface design that could support the VLABS proposal, not a confirmed vacancy or employment offer. It is not an open position.

## Design Work This Role Could Support

- Research how learners, teachers and administrators prepare for tasks, recognise difficulty and decide what comes next.
- Map the path from learning goal to practical choice, recorded observation and teacher review.
- Prototype clear instructions, feedback and recovery states, making planned AI limits and teacher controls visible.
- Test keyboard access, readable contrast and assistive-technology behaviour across representative learning devices.
- Work with education and engineering specialists to challenge usability and feasibility against pilot feedback before expansion.

## Professional Experience to Discuss

Relevant experience includes user research, interaction design, prototyping, design systems and accessibility evaluation, with examples of choices revised after testing. Any tool requirements, responsibilities and qualifications must be confirmed in an actual role description.
