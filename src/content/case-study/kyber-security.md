overview:
  title: "Start With the Warning on the Screen"
  content: |
    In this illustrative task, a simulated sensor warning gives trainees a question to investigate. What might happen if the warning threshold changes? They hold the input value fixed, record what appears and explain why they chose that test.

    The VLABS proposal provides the design direction for linking these activities with assessment evidence. This is not a customer implementation, a confirmed integration or a verified training result. The content and tools for any pilot need separate confirmation.
challenges:
  title: "Ask Why That Setting Was Chosen"
  content: |
    A trainee may adjust a setting until the warning disappears and still be unsure what caused it. The instructor can ask them to talk through the choice. A clear test record and common criteria make that conversation about the reasoning behind the action.

    Simulation can support preparation and repeated practice, but its accuracy and limits must be reviewed for the training objective. It does not replace supervised equipment use, safety instruction or professional certification requirements.
solution:
  title: "Keep the Choice Beside the Observation"
  content: "In this task idea, the instructor looks at what trainees tested and how they explain it. The next question can focus on understanding the threshold, following the procedure or both."
  items:
    - "Agree what trainees should investigate and how their starting point will be recorded. Choose preparation materials and a suitable simulated sensor task."
    - "Ask what trainees expect the warning to do. Explain the threshold variable, fixed input and simulation limits before independent practice."
    - "Try the task with a changed threshold. Record the input, setting and warning response, then explain the reason for each test choice."
    - "Read the test record alongside the explanation. Talk through whether the trainee needs help with the threshold, the procedure or both."
    - "Choose focused support and gather fresh evidence against the same criteria. Review the experience before considering a wider programme."
evaluation:
  title: "Talk Through How the Test Was Done"
  content: "Compare with the agreed baseline, using educational and operational criteria. This scenario promises no productivity, revenue, certification or learning improvement."
  items:
    - "Can trainees explain the connection between the threshold change, fixed input and warning they observed?"
    - "Do the test records and instructors' judgements follow the agreed assessment criteria?"
    - "Does the support address the difficulty with the concept or procedure, taking the training setting and learner's starting point into account?"
    - "What do trainees and instructors need in terms of usability, accessibility, data quality and support?"
