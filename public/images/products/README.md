# VLABS product screenshots

Captured on 2026-11-15 from the real demo application at
`https://vlabs.learningsmart.io.vn`, using the student and teacher access
authorised for this website content update. Screenshots show the Vietnamese
interface. All files are 1440 × 960 WebP images.

| File | View | Source route |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `vlabs-projectile-lab.webp` | 3D projectile experiment and its guided investigation | `/lab/projectile-motion-3d` |

| `vlabs-teacher-dashboard.webp` | Teacher class summary, marking queue and lesson area | `/teacher` |

Account names and avatars were permanently covered with opaque privacy masks
before exporting the website assets. No account emails, passwords, access
tokens or named learner rosters are included. Raw captures and browser session
data are not website assets.

The interface, simulation readings and activity figures are genuine demo
content, not customer results or evidence of improved learning outcomes. Empty
states were retained; lessons, tests, grades and rosters were not edited to
stage screenshots. Use captions that identify these as demo product views,
not photographs of the team or institutional deployments.

These views establish what was visible in the demo, not a guarantee of feature
availability, subject coverage, accessibility, integration support or service
terms in every deployment. Confirm those requirements with the VLABS team.
